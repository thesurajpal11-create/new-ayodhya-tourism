Overview

Visit Prayagraj, the sacred land of Triveni Sangam, where three holy rivers meet. Our tour package is designed for those seeking spiritual peace and meaningful travel. The journey includes Sangam visit, temple darshan, and comfortable road travel from Ayodhya. We manage timing, transport, and basic arrangements so you can focus on your experience. Prayagraj is especially significant for religious rituals and family visits. With a simple and well-managed plan, this tour ensures a calm and respectful travel experience suitable for all age groups.

The city is often visited as part of an Ayodhya Prayagraj Varanasi route because the three destinations create a strong spiritual triangle in Uttar Pradesh. A private cab makes the route easier for families and senior citizens, especially when guests want to avoid confusion around parking, ghats and temple areas. We help with pickup, route planning, local stops and practical timing for a holy dip at Sangam where possible.

Prayagraj can be covered in one day if you start early, but a slower two day package is better for visitors who want Sangam, Bade Hanuman Ji, Anand Bhavan, Alopi Devi Mandir, local temples and nearby heritage points without rushing. During Magh Mela and Kumbh related periods, planning becomes more important because roads, stay options and crowds change quickly. Our team keeps the itinerary realistic and communicates clearly before the journey.

Best Time To Visit

October to March is the best time to visit Prayagraj for comfortable weather and easier local movement. Magh Mela and Kumbh periods are spiritually powerful but need advance planning for hotels, cab routes and pickup points. Summer visits should be planned with early morning Sangam time and afternoon rest.

Suggested Itinerary

Day 1: Sangam And Temple Visits

Pickup from hotel, station or Ayodhya/Varanasi route, followed by Triveni Sangam, Bade Hanuman Ji and nearby temple visits.

Afternoon: Heritage Stops

Day 2: Relaxed Darshan

Add extra Sangam time, local market visit or nearby temple route before departure.

Optional Extension
