Overview

Explore the spiritual heart of India with our Ayodhya tour package designed for a peaceful and well-organized journey. Ayodhya, known as the birthplace of Lord Ram and Ram Mandir, attracts millions of devotees every year. Our package covers Ram Mandir, Hanuman Garhi, Dashrath Mahal, Kanak Bhawan, Saryu Ghat aarti, and nearby Lata Mangeshkar Chowk with smooth local transport and guided support. We focus on comfort, timing, and darshan convenience so that your visit remains stress-free. Whether you are travelling with family or elders, our flexible plans ensure a complete devotional experience with reliable hotel and cab arrangements.

The city is closely connected with Bhagwan Shri Ram, so visitors usually want more than a quick sightseeing round. They want enough time for Ram Mandir darshan, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Saryu Ghat, Ram Ki Paidi and other sacred stops without rushing from one place to another. Our Ayodhya package is designed around comfort, route clarity and easy communication, especially for guests arriving by train, flight or road from Lucknow, Varanasi, Prayagraj and nearby cities.

You can book a one day Ayodhya local tour, a two day relaxed darshan plan, or a multi city spiritual circuit combining Ayodhya with Varanasi, Prayagraj, Chitrakoot, Mathura or Vrindavan. The package can include pickup, local sightseeing cab, hotel coordination and onward travel planning. We keep the language simple, the plan flexible and the support personal, so your family can focus on the spiritual journey.

Best Time To Visit

October to March is the most comfortable season for Ayodhya sightseeing because the weather is pleasant for temple queues and ghat visits. Many devotees also travel during Ram Navami, Diwali, Kartik Purnima and festival periods, but those dates need early hotel and cab planning because demand rises sharply. Summer trips are possible with morning and evening sightseeing, while monsoon visits feel peaceful if road and rain timing is considered.

Suggested Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival And Core Darshan

Pickup from Ayodhya station, airport or hotel, followed by Ram Mandir darshan planning, Hanuman Garhi, Kanak Bhawan, Dashrath Mahal and nearby temple visits.

Evening: Saryu Ghat Experience

Visit Saryu Ghat or Ram Ki Paidi for a peaceful evening, photography, aarti atmosphere and local market time if your schedule allows.

Day 2: Relaxed Local Sightseeing

Add Guptar Ghat, Nageshwarnath Temple, nearby spiritual places and free time for repeat darshan or shopping before departure.

Optional Extension

Continue by cab to Prayagraj, Varanasi, Chitrakoot, Vindhyachal, Mathura or Vrindavan with hotel support on request.

FAQs

How many days are enough for Ayodhya?

One full day covers the main darshan points, while two days are better for a relaxed family trip with ghats and nearby places.

Can you arrange cab and hotel together?

Yes, we can help with cab service and hotel options based on budget, group size and preferred location.

Is Ayodhya suitable for senior citizens?

Yes. A private cab, nearby hotel and slower itinerary make the journey easier for senior citizens.

Can Ayodhya be combined with Varanasi or Prayagraj?

Yes, Ayodhya combines well with Varanasi, Prayagraj, Chitrakoot and Vindhyachal by road.
