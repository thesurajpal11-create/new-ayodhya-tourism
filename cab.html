FAQs

Cab Booking Questions

Which cab options are listed?

The page lists Sedan, Kia Car, SUV, Innova, and Tempo Traveller. The route estimator provides fare cards for Sedan, SUV, Innova, and Tempo Traveller.

How do I get an estimate for my route?

Choose a pickup, destination, trip type, and whether you are travelling solo or with family, then select Get Estimate. Routes without a listed distance use an approximate estimate that should be confirmed with the team.

Are the displayed fares final?

Route estimates are indicative. The existing estimate notes that toll, parking, and night charges may be extra if applicable; use the Call or WhatsApp option to discuss your trip and confirm the fare.

Can I choose a pickup date?

Yes. The pickup date field is optional. The planner accepts today or a future date.

How do I discuss or confirm a cab?

After viewing the route estimate, use its Chat on WhatsApp or Call Now button to contact the team. The WhatsApp action opens a message for you to send; it does not send an automatic notification when you select a route.
