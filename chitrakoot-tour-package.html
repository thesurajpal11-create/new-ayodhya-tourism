Overview

Chitrakoot is known for its deep connection with Lord Ram's exile period and peaceful natural surroundings. Our tour package focuses on creating a calm and well-managed travel experience. Key locations like Ram Ghat, Kamadgiri Parikrama, and other sacred spots are included in the plan. We provide reliable transport, simple hotel options, and local guidance for a smooth journey. This destination is perfect for those looking for a quiet spiritual trip away from crowded cities. With proper planning and support, your Chitrakoot visit becomes comfortable and fulfilling.

Unlike very crowded city routes, Chitrakoot has a slower devotional rhythm. Guests often prefer time for Ram Ghat, evening atmosphere, Kamadgiri Parikrama route, temple visits and nearby natural sites. A private cab helps because places are spread across different directions and local planning saves time. We can arrange pickup from Ayodhya, Prayagraj, Varanasi, Lucknow or nearby railway stations based on your route.

Chitrakoot works well as a two day trip and also combines beautifully with Ayodhya and Prayagraj. Senior citizens, families and small groups appreciate a measured itinerary with rest time, simple hotel options and clear pickup coordination. Ramnagari Tourism keeps the package practical and respectful, helping you focus on darshan and the quiet spiritual feel of the place.

Best Time To Visit

October to March is the most pleasant season for Chitrakoot because sightseeing, parikrama and temple visits are easier in mild weather. Monsoon adds greenery and beauty, though route timing should be checked. Summer travel is possible with early starts and rest breaks.

Suggested Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival And Ram Ghat

Pickup from station, hotel or nearby city and visit Ram Ghat, Bharat Milap, Janaki Kund and evening ghat time.

Day 2: Sacred Sites

Cover Kamadgiri, Sati Anusuya, Gupt Godavari, Hanuman Dhara and nearby devotional places.

Flexible Return

Optional Extension

Add Prayagraj Sangam or Ayodhya Ram Mandir for a complete Ramayan inspired route.
